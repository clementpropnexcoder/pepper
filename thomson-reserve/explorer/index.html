51,567 sqm of garden living beside the Central Catchment

Thomson Reserve rises on the former Thomson View Condominium site on Bright Hill Drive: 1,268 homes in six towers, set among water gardens and mature landscape. Upper Thomson MRT is a two-minute sheltered walk from the main gate, Ai Tong School is within 1 km, and MacRitchie Reservoir and Windsor Nature Park are moments away.

Homes range from a 592 sqft 2-bedroom to the 1,808 sqft 5-bedroom suite with private lift. Explore every one of them in the 3D model above.

Location

Nature, schools and the MRT, all at one address

Thomson Reserve sits where Upper Thomson meets the Central Catchment: more than 2,000 hectares of protected forest on one side, the Upper Thomson dining belt and Thomson Plaza on the other.

Connectivity

  • Upper Thomson MRT (TEL) — 2-minute sheltered walk via a pedestrian gateway at the main entrance
  • Direct on the TEL to Orchard, Stevens, Outram Park, Shenton Way, Marina Bay and Gardens by the Bay
  • Circle Line at Caldecott, one stop away; future Cross Island Line at Bright Hill
  • CTE & PIE minutes away by car

Schools

  • Ai Tong School — within 1 km, for Phase 2C Primary 1 priority
  • Within 2 km: Catholic High School, CHIJ St Nicholas Girls', Marymount Convent, Eunoia JC

Nature

Why it works

A city-fringe address on a land cost below recent suburban sites

  • Lower land cost. The $810 million en bloc works out to $1,178 psf ppr, below recent OCR land sales such as Chuan Grove, Lentor Central and Bedok Rise, despite the city-fringe location.
  • Three established developers. UOL, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development, the team behind Skye at Holland and Parktown Residence.
  • Scale. 1,268 homes on a 51,567 sqm site support a large facility set while spreading upkeep across many owners, and give more transactions to benchmark resale prices.
  • Lasting greenery. Nature-facing homes look over the protected Central Catchment, which cannot be built over.
  • Future upside. The Cross Island Line station at Bright Hill adds a third line within reach.
